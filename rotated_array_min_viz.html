Why Right Partition Test Works 100% of the Time

* If keys[mid] < keys[high]: The right segment [mid..high] goes steadily uphill. No cliff drop exists on the right! Therefore, the minimum element MUST be in the left half (including mid).

* If keys[mid] > keys[high]: The middle value is BIGGER than the end value. A cliff drop MUST have occurred between mid and high! Therefore, the minimum element MUST be in the right half (mid + 1).
